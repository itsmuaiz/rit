# Rit 🚗

**Track your work trips. Navigate with ease. Keep your mileage organized.**

Rit is a mobile mileage tracker that makes business travel easier to record. Start a trip with a single tap, follow your route on the map, and keep your business trips together in one clear overview.

## ✨ Key Features

- **One-tap tracking** — Start and stop GPS mileage recording with a single button.
- **Live trip stats** — View your distance traveled and trip duration while driving.
- **Interactive map** — See your current location and recorded route.
- **In-app navigation** — Search for destinations and follow turn-by-turn directions.
- **Trip history** — Browse saved trips with distances, dates, and departure and arrival locations.
- **Business descriptions** — Add a purpose or note to each trip.
- **Mileage summaries** — View totals by week, month, or a custom date range.
- **CSV export** — Export trip records for expense reporting and administration.
- **Local storage** — Keep saved trips available between app launches.
- **Light and dark modes** — Enjoy a clean interface in either appearance.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Open Rit and tap **Start Trip**.
2. Optionally choose a destination to enable navigation.
3. Drive while Rit records your route and mileage.
4. Tap **Stop Trip** when you arrive.
5. Review the details, add an optional description, and save.
6. Find your saved trip in the **Trips** tab.

## 📍 Trip Details

Each saved trip includes:

- Departure and arrival locations
- Actual distance traveled
- Date and start time
- End time and total duration
- Recorded route on a map
- Optional business description

Recorded mileage is based on your actual GPS track, independently of the estimated navigation distance.

## 🎨 Design

Rit follows Apple-inspired design principles:

- Clear typography and generous spacing
- Familiar navigation and native interaction patterns
- Subtle translucent panels above the map
- Immediate button feedback and meaningful haptics
- Smooth, interruptible animations
- Large, accessible touch targets
- Support for larger text, screen readers, and reduced motion

## 🛠️ Tracking and Reliability

The app is designed to support:

- Background recording during active trips, subject to device permissions and operating system restrictions
- GPS noise filtering to reduce inaccurate mileage
- Periodic saving of active trips to help recover from interruptions
- Clear messages for weak GPS signals or missing permissions
- Continued GPS recording during temporary internet outages
- Address lookup when connectivity becomes available again

## 🔒 Privacy

Location access is used for trip recording and navigation. Background location permission allows active trips to continue recording while the screen is locked or another app is open.

Location recording stops when a trip ends. Trip records are stored locally on the device.

## 🌍 Language and Units

- **Interface:** Dutch
- **Distance:** Kilometers
- **Dates:** Dutch date formatting
- **Time:** 24-hour format

Rit is under development. The features listed above describe the intended product; availability depends on the current build.
